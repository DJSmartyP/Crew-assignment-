HOW THE CREW SUGGESTION WORKS

1. First come, first served
If two people are otherwise equally suited to the same choice, the person who chose first gets priority.

2. The crew list can change
Assignments are only the best suggestion based on the choices received so far. When a new person adds their preferences, or somebody changes theirs, the whole crew suggestion is worked out again.

3. We try to accommodate everybody
The tracker tries 1st choices first, then 2nd, then 3rd, then flexible/no-preference options. It also tries to keep people away from roles they marked Really don't want.

4. Nothing is permanently claimed while choices are coming in
A station showing FILLED FOR NOW is not locked to that person. Suggested assignments may move until everyone has given their preferences.

5. The tracker is there to help, not decide for you
The suggested assignment is not something anyone HAS to follow. It is a tool to help build two balanced crews.

6. Need help?
If something looks wrong, you cannot edit your choices, or you need a change, contact Smarty or Sarah.
